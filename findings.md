# Key Findings

Based on 148,541 Swiggy restaurant listings (61,441 of them rated).

## 1. Listings are concentrated in a few locations
Bikaner (1,666 listings) and Noida-1 (1,428) have the most restaurants of any single location. They are followed by neighbourhoods of Delhi, Bangalore and Pune: Indirapuram (1,279), BTM (1,161), Rohini (1,136), Kothrud (1,089) and Indiranagar (1,080).

## 2. North Indian and Chinese dominate the menu
The most common cuisine listing is North Indian + Chinese (6,471 restaurants), followed closely by Indian (6,414) and Chinese (5,051). South Indian (3,303) and Bakery (3,132) are also in the top seven.

## 3. Pizza and ice cream chains have the widest reach
Domino's has the most branches (442), ahead of Pizza Hut (319) and KFC (309). Kwality Wall's (300) and Baskin Robbins (274) show how large the ice cream and dessert segment is.

## 4. Ice cream and desserts are the best rated cuisines
Ice Cream + Desserts has the highest average rating (4.36 across 1,046 rated restaurants), followed by Ice Cream (4.32) and Sweets + Desserts (4.25). The top-rated, high-volume restaurants are also mostly ice cream and sweet shops (NIC Natural Ice Creams, Corner House Ice Cream, Vijay Dairy, The Grand Sweets and Snacks), all at 4.7.

## 5. Premium areas cost much more, but ratings barely differ
Khan Market, Delhi is the most expensive location (average ₹601), ahead of Fort Colaba, Mumbai (₹493) and North Goa (₹469). Among locations with 50+ rated restaurants, the top five ratings sit between 4.21 and 4.23 (Mylapore, South Kolkata, Frazer Town, Adyar, Mahalaxmi Malabar Hill), so higher prices do not mean noticeably higher ratings.

## 6. Chennai gives the best value
Mylapore (4.23 rating, average ₹296) and Adyar (4.22, ₹319) in Chennai match the best-rated areas in the country at a lower average cost than Mumbai's top-rated areas (Mahalaxmi Malabar Hill: 4.21, ₹410).
